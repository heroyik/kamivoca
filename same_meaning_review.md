## Kept With Meaning Differentiation

### 配慮がある / 思いやりがある

- Decision: `keep both`
- Reason: Korean glosses had collapsed them into the same meaning, but the nuance is different.
- `配慮がある`: more about being considerate, careful, and taking the other person's situation into account.
- `思いやりがある`: more about warm-hearted empathy and human kindness.
